# Foundations

- Cieslak and Pang, “Common Shocks in Stocks and Bonds,” Journal of Financial Economics 2021. Joint stock-yield behavior helps distinguish growth, monetary-policy, common-premium and hedging-premium shocks: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2021.06.008>
- Lou, Polk and Skouras, “A Tug of War: Overnight versus Intraday Expected Returns,” Journal of Financial Economics 2019. Overnight and regular-session returns can reflect different investor clienteles and mechanisms: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2019.03.011>

System rule: classify the cross-asset mechanism and price absorption separately. Never map an overnight move directly to the open-to-close direction.

# 综合判断

Use the six reports and adversary as the complete information set. The target is official US regular-session open to close; already-realized premarket returns are not that target.

## Method

1. Identify the leading company-specific mechanism and the market/industry background.
2. Compare continuation and reversal explanations, including what may already be priced in.
3. Explain why one mechanism is more persuasive despite its strongest countercase, or reject when neither has a defensible basis.
4. Neutral market context is not a required positive vote. Ordinary disagreement is not an integrity veto. Do not count reports or raw roots as confidence.
5. Select at most the configured cap. Compare chosen candidates with the alternatives using mechanisms, not a numerical confidence score. An empty selection is allowed.

## Output

For every candidate state: action, direction, thesis, antithesis, resolution, comparison, unknowns, invalidation and evidence_ids. Cite only available reports. Missing observations remain missing; do not browse or add remembered facts.

The program validates source references, timing, integrity and the publication cap. This is a paper research version, with no broker access. See [foundations](references/foundations.md).

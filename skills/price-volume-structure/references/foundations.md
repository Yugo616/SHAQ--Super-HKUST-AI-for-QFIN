# Foundations

- Murray, Xia and Xiao, “Charting by Machines,” Journal of Financial Economics 2024. Historical price paths can contain stable nonlinearities and interactions beyond isolated technical rules: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2024.103791>
- Sullivan, Timmermann and White, “Data-Snooping, Technical Trading Rule Performance, and the Bootstrap,” Journal of Finance 1999. Searching large collections of chart rules creates material selection bias: <https://doi.org/10.1111/0022-1082.00163>
- Futu's historical-candlestick contract supplies explicitly unadjusted regular-session OHLCV through the configured adapter; captured provider rows remain immutable evidence rather than model-generated features: <https://openapi.futunn.com/futu-api-doc/en/quote/request-history-kline.html>

System rule: classify a readable open-to-close state from event type, residual gap, participation and liquidity. Never map a single indicator or gap mechanically to direction.

# Foundations

- Pan and Poteshman, “The Information in Option Volume for Future Stock Prices,” Review of Financial Studies 2006. The signal uses buyer-initiated opening volume, not raw call or put volume: <https://doi.org/10.1093/rfs/hhj024>
- Cremers and Weinbaum, “Deviations from Put-Call Parity and Stock Return Predictability,” Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis 2010. The measure compares matched call and put implied volatilities: <https://doi.org/10.1017/S002210901000013X>
- Futu OpenAPI option-event contract provides active side, order classification, strategy type, turnover and volume/open-interest context: <https://openapi.futunn.com/futu-api-doc/quote/get-option-event.html>

System rule: describe the implied distribution first. Permit direction only when trade initiation and position semantics are reliable; raw open interest and put/call labels are insufficient.

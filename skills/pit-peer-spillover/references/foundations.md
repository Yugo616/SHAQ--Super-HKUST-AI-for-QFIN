# Foundations

- Hoberg and Phillips, “Text-Based Network Industries and Endogenous Product Differentiation,” Journal of Political Economy 2016. Dynamic product-market networks replace fixed industry membership: <https://doi.org/10.1086/688176>
- Cohen and Frazzini, “Economic Links and Predictable Returns,” Journal of Finance 2008. Information can propagate with delay through customer-supplier links: <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2008.01379.x>

System rule: require point-in-time named relationships. Let the relationship type determine the transmission sign; use ETF and correlation evidence only as confirmation.
